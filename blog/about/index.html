Our editorial approach

Useful details.
Clear sources.

These guides are published by Penta AI, a website design inspection product. They explain how to inspect fonts, colors, CSS and design systems, and how to turn observations into useful work.

Who these guides are for

Designers who want to understand a reference, developers who need to inspect an interface, and builders who use AI coding tools and need a more precise design brief.

How we explain a workflow

We favor concrete steps, worked examples and links to primary documentation. Sample values and code illustrate a method; they are not measurements of a named website unless the guide says so. Browser interfaces and third-party tools can change, so verify the current documentation when a step looks different.

What we disclose

Penta AI is the publisher and benefits when readers choose the product. Product links are clearly labeled. Educational workflows can also use browser tools. We do not present hypothetical examples as customer results, invent authors or testimonials, or claim firsthand testing without describing it.

About our visuals

Covers are original interface illustrations inspired by the visual examples on the Penta AI homepage. They illustrate inspection concepts and are not live product screenshots. Diagrams are original explanatory visuals with illustrative values and layouts.

Sources and maintenance

Material technical claims link to the documentation that supports them. Dates describe when a guide was prepared or published; they do not establish that a tool is unchanged. Substantive corrections should update the guide and its modification date.

Using a reference responsibly

Inspection helps you understand how an interface works. Reuse your own material, licensed assets, or material you have permission to use. A visible image, logo, font or implementation is not automatically available for republication.
