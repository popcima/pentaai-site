Explain Abbreviations Without Depending on a Hover Tooltip

Use abbr for abbreviation semantics, provide unfamiliar expansions in visible text, and keep explanations available on touch and keyboard devices.

By Penta AIPublished 21 November 20261 min read

In this guide

  1. Expand the term where it first matters
  2. Use title as supplementary information
  3. Review the paragraph without pointer hover

An abbreviation’s meaning should not depend on a reader discovering a browser tooltip. Use abbr when the semantics are useful, and explain an unfamiliar term in visible text at an appropriate point in the reading flow.

Expand the term where it first matters

<p>
  Document Object Model (<abbr>DOM</abbr>) inspection helps you
  connect a visible component with its rendered markup.
</p>

The expansion is available without hovering, focusing or guessing. The abbr element marks the shortened form; it does not require a title attribute. Later uses can stay concise when the context remains clear. In a long reference page, a visible glossary link can be more useful than repeating the full expansion in every sentence.

Reference: MDN abbr.

Use title as supplementary information

When title is used on abbr, its content should be the full expansion or description. Each occurrence stands on its own: adding a title to one DOM abbreviation does not automatically define every other occurrence on the page.

Browser tooltip access and assistive-technology announcement behavior vary. Do not make essential instructions available only through title. Avoid turning every familiar acronym into a keyboard stop just to imitate a tooltip; that can make ordinary reading unnecessarily cumbersome.

Review the paragraph without pointer hover

Read the page on a touch device and navigate it with the keyboard. Confirm that a newcomer can understand the term from the visible sentence. Inspect any dotted underline or custom styling to ensure it does not falsely suggest a link. If the abbreviation has several meanings, use the expansion appropriate to this exact context.

Keep language markup accurate when the expansion contains a meaningful language change. See the language-markup guide and the tooltip review when supplementary help becomes an interactive component.
