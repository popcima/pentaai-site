Find Keyboard Focus Hidden Behind Sticky Headers

Test focus beneath sticky headers, footers and banners. Fix overlapping page chrome and verify forward and backward navigation at enlarged sizes.

By Penta AIPublished 16 November 20262 min read

In this guide

  1. Reproduce the overlap with real focus
  2. Repair the overlap at its source
  3. Check the paths after a repair

A visible focus style is not enough if a sticky header or floating banner covers the focused control. Review keyboard navigation through the complete page, especially near the viewport edges. The user needs to see where their next action will occur.

Reproduce the overlap with real focus

Open the page with its normal header, cookie notice and any persistent action bar. Tab through controls until one approaches an edge, then reverse with Shift+Tab. Repeat at a smaller viewport and with enlarged text. A click on the control may scroll differently from sequential keyboard navigation, so test both deliberately.

Reference: W3C Focus Not Obscured (Minimum) explanation.

The Level AA minimum concerns a focused component being entirely hidden by author-created content. Aim for the whole control to remain visible where practical; partial visibility is a minimum boundary, not an ideal design target. Review the criterion’s notes when user-positioned or user-opened content is involved.

Repair the overlap at its source

html { scroll-padding-block-start: 5rem; }
.section-target { scroll-margin-block-start: 5rem; }

These illustrative offsets can help some scrolling and target-alignment paths, but they are not a universal keyboard-focus fix. Verify the actual browser behavior and header size. A header that wraps to two lines can outgrow a fixed offset, and a nested scrolling panel may need its own treatment.

Consider reducing sticky content, allowing it to leave the viewport at high zoom or reserving enough usable space. A permanent footer plus a large header can leave very little working area even when neither one is individually excessive. Do not solve an overlap by removing the focus indicator.

Check the paths after a repair

  • Tab forward and backward near both viewport edges.
  • Activate skip links and error-summary links.
  • Open and close persistent notices and nonmodal panels.
  • Increase text size until header labels wrap.
  • Inspect controls inside nested scroll containers.

In the issue report, name the focused element, covering element, viewport and exact navigation sequence. “Focus is missing” can mean several different defects; a screenshot showing the overlap plus the active element narrows the diagnosis. Use the anchor-spacing workflow for fragment alignment, then separately verify keyboard focus visibility.
