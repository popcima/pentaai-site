Penta AI is a live preview for inspecting this page’s colors, fonts and design tokens.

The live preview reads this page’s computed colors and fonts, lets you copy values, and exports CSS variables, JSON tokens or a DESIGN.md brief. The broader extension features shown here are product concepts.

Not at all. Open the live demo, choose Colors, Type or Export, and explore the values. It is built for designers, no-code builders and developers alike.

The product concept covers images, SVGs, icons, videos and Lottie files. This website preview exports design tokens and a design brief; bulk asset extraction is not enabled.

The working demo gathers computed color and font values from this page and organizes them into CSS, JSON or Markdown. Full Tailwind, shadcn/ui and DTCG exports are shown as product concepts.

The demo reads styles, copies values and generates files locally in your browser. It does not upload page content or save your inspection results to a server.

Page cloning is part of the proposed product. This preview does not clone external websites or export their source. You can download this page’s observed design values.
